= Model Training
:navtitle: Model Training
:imagesdir: ../images

The Model Training demo walks through fine-tuning a model on Intel hardware.
You will configure a training job, monitor its progress, and compare the fine-tuned model against the base model.

== What You Will Learn

* How to set up a fine-tuning job on Intel Gaudi
* How to monitor training progress
* How fine-tuning changes model behavior for a specific task

== Try It: Configure a Training Job

. Click the *Demo* tab and navigate to the *Training* page.
. Select the base model: *Granite 3.2 8B Instruct*.
. Choose a training dataset from the available options (e.g., customer support Q&A).
. Configure training parameters:
** *Epochs:* 3
** *Learning rate:* 2e-5
** *Batch size:* 4
. Click *Start Training*.
. *Expected result* — a training job launches. The progress panel shows loss curves, learning rate schedule, and estimated time remaining.

== Try It: Monitor Training Progress

During training, the dashboard shows:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Metric |What It Shows

|Training loss
|How well the model fits the training data — should decrease over epochs

|Validation loss
|How well the model generalizes — should track training loss without diverging

|Learning rate
|Current learning rate value following the configured schedule

|Throughput
|Samples processed per second on Intel Gaudi
|===

== Try It: Compare Base vs Fine-Tuned

After training completes:

. The demo provides a side-by-side comparison interface.
. Enter a prompt relevant to the training dataset:
+
[source]
----
"A customer reports they cannot access their account after a password reset. What troubleshooting steps should I follow?"
----
. *Expected result* — the base model gives a generic response. The fine-tuned model gives a response tailored to the specific support workflow from the training data, with domain-specific terminology and procedures.
