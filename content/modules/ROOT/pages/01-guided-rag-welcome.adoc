= Guided RAG on Intel Xeon
:navtitle: Welcome

Welcome to your personalized Guided RAG lab. The instructions stay on the left while your own live environment opens in the tabs on the right.

== What you will build

You will run a grounded question through four observable stages:

. Embed the question on Intel Xeon.
. Retrieve relevant knowledge.
. Rerank the candidate passages.
. Generate an answer backed by the retrieved context.

== Verify access

. Select the *RAG Workspace* tab.
. Confirm that the Enterprise RAG page loads.
. Select the *Terminal* tab.
. Run the following command:
+
[source,bash,role=execute]
----
oc project {namespace}
----
. Confirm that OpenShift reports your assigned namespace.

If either tab fails, stop and ask the instructor to retry this seat. Do not continue using another participant's URL.
