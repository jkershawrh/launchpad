= Scale One Workload Dimension at a Time

== Baseline, Sustained, Pressure

[cols="1,1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Profile | Agent replicas | Concurrent journeys | Purpose
| Baseline | 1 | 1 | Establish the known-good reference
| Sustained | 2 | 5 | Measure normal parallel operation
| Pressure | 3 | 10 | Find the first bounded constraint
|===

Changing every dimension at once hides causality. First increase replicas while
holding concurrency constant. Then increase concurrency. Finally observe the
resulting pressure on the shared inference endpoint.

At every checkpoint, verify readiness, journey correlation, queue depth, error
rate, p50/p95 latency, and policy evaluation. Stop rather than advancing if the
current profile is not understood.

NOTE: This is workload scale inside one namespace. Launchpad's separate
1-, 5-, and 25-participant delivery certification is not a learner exercise.
