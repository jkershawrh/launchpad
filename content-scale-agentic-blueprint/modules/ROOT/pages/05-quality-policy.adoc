= Protect Quality and Policy Under Load

== Compare the Same Cases

Run the immutable cases under sustained and pressure profiles. Compare each
result with its baseline case rather than comparing unrelated requests.

Performance alone cannot pass this lab. The workload must preserve supported
decisions, complete evidence provenance, deterministic policy attribution,
review-required state, and zero unsupported claims. A faster answer with weaker
evidence is a regression.

== Review the Exceptions

Inspect every denied and inconclusive case plus a representative sample of
supported cases. Confirm that policy denial remains deterministic and that
missing evidence produces an inconclusive result or human escalation—not a
confident model guess.

== Quality Checkpoint

You can show whether quality or policy changed as load increased and identify
the exact case and architecture boundary responsible for any regression.
